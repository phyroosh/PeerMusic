## Cover Architecture & Editorial Decisions

| Field | Required Decision |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Editorial idea** | **The Dawn Summit & The Soaring Flight:** A solitary, unburdened silhouette standing atop a mist-swept mountain precipice at the exact moment dawn breaks. With arms open wide to the sky, a magnificent arc of radiant white birds/origami cranes soars upward into volumetric golden sunbeams, symbolizing the complete release of self-punishment and the euphoric arrival of peace (*"Aaj haath khol diye hain... Sukoon... bas sukoon hai"*). |
| **Focal hierarchy** | 1. **First:** The blazing golden-hour sun rising over the jagged mountain ridge, throwing volumetric light rays through mountain mist.<br>2. **Second:** The soaring dynamic flock of luminous white cranes arcing gracefully across the sky.<br>3. **Third:** The monumental Didone serif title **SUKOON** in champagne ivory (`#FAF4E8`) with warm-gold rim aura and poetic italic subtitle *"Maine Khud Ko Maaf Kar Diya"* (`#E5B869`). |
| **Palette and material** | - **Palette:** Deep Obsidian Navy (`#060913`, `#0D1527`), Midnight Sapphire (`#1E293B`), Warm Royal Amber (`#D97706`, `#F59E0B`), Blazing Sunrise Gold (`#FEF08A`, `#FFFBEB`), and Signature PeerMusic Crimson (`#DC2626`, `#FF1E27`).<br>- **Lighting:** Majestic back-lit sunrise casting long chiaroscuro rim highlights onto mountain ridges, mist valleys, and the silhouette.<br>- **Material:** Geometric layered mountain ridges, volumetric sunbeam shafts, fluttering origami crane wings, and floating golden dust motes. |
| **Type layout** | - **16:9 Widescreen:** Asymmetric left-aligned negative-space typography field (`x: 140, y: 180`). The sunrise and mountain crest anchor the right two-thirds, while the left field houses the Didone title **SUKOON** (120px) and poetic italic subtitle.<br>- **1:1 Square:** Top-centered masthead arrangement. The rising sun and mountain crest anchor the bottom-center (Y: 65%), while the cranes fly upward framing the monumental centered title. |
| **16:9 composition** | Native 1920×1080 canvas. Left-to-right visual flow: eye starts on the bold title on the left, travels across the soaring flock of cranes in the center sky, and lands on the sunlit silhouette atop the mountain peak on the right. |
| **1:1 composition** | Native 1500×1500 canvas. Symmetrical vertical majesty: mountain summit grounded at the base, sunburst blooming behind the silhouette, birds fanning upward into the open upper-atmosphere, and high-fashion centered editorial masthead. |
| **Brand treatment** | Official circular PeerMusic badge (`PeerMusicLogoBadge`) anchored in the upper corner with subtle red-and-gold breathing aura. |
