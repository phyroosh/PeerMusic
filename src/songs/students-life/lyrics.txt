Title: Class Ke Baad (Student Life Anthem)

[Intro]
(Bright acoustic guitar, campus ambience, light claps and a distant college bell)

[Mukhda / Chorus]
Class ke baad jo zindagi thi, woh class mein kahan milti thi,
Ek bench pe chhoti duniya thi, har baat nayi si lagti thi.
Notes adhoore, sapne poore, aankhon mein shehar basaaya tha,
Kal ka kya hai, aaj doston ne, hans ke humein sikhaaya tha.
Class ke baad jo zindagi thi, woh class mein kahan milti thi.

[Antara 1 / Verse 1]
Subah ki bus mein neend sambhaale, bag bhi aadha khula tha,
Gate pe milte hi lagta tha, din apna hi likha tha.
Roll call mein naam pukara, kisi ne “present” keh diya,
Ek tiffin ke chaar hisse the, bhookh ka bhi fest kar diya.
Canteen ki udhaar wali chai, kitni mehngi yaad bani,
Assignment ki deadline se zyada, dosti humko khaas lagi.
Teacher ke sawaalon se bachkar, khidki se badal dekhe the,
Chhote-chhote breaks mein humne, bade-bade kal dekhe the.

[Pre-Chorus]
Kisi ko ghar ki yaad sataati, koi result se darta tha,
Phir bhi “ho jaayega” kehne wala, paas hi koi rehta tha.

[Mukhda / Chorus]
Class ke baad jo zindagi thi, woh class mein kahan milti thi,
Ek bench pe chhoti duniya thi, har baat nayi si lagti thi.
Notes adhoore, sapne poore, aankhon mein shehar basaaya tha,
Kal ka kya hai, aaj doston ne, hans ke humein sikhaaya tha.

[Antara 2 / Verse 2]
Hostel ki chhat pe teen baje, future par behas chali,
Koi startup, koi music, koi ghar lautne ki baat chali.
Maa ko bola “sab theek hai,” jab jeb mein bas sikke the,
Phir bhi birthday cake ke tukde, sabke hisse barabar the.
Fail hue toh saath baithkar, marks ko humne hasa diya,
Jeet kisi ek ki hoti thi, par poore group ne mana diya.
Degree ke panno se pehle, hum khud ko padhna seekh gaye,
Gir kar uthna, haath badhaana, ek-dooje se seekh gaye.

[Bridge]
Ek din yeh corridor khaali, aur yeh timetable puraana hoga,
Jo roz milte the bina bulaaye, unse milna bahaana hoga.
Par photo mein jo shor bacha hai, woh kabhi kam nahi hoga,
Hum alag shehron mein honge, par apna campus saath hoga.

[Final Chorus / Outro]
Class ke baad jo zindagi thi, woh class mein kahan milti thi,
Har dost ki ek awaaz mein, apni hi dhadkan chalti thi.
Notes adhoore, sapne poore, raaste naye banaayenge,
Ghanti baj kar keh bhi de toh, yaadon mein phir mil jaayenge.
Class ke baad jo zindagi thi...
Woh hum mein hamesha zinda thi.
(Acoustic guitar rings out; friends laughing fades into a final campus bell.)
