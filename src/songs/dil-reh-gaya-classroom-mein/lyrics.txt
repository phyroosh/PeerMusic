Title: Dil Reh Gaya Classroom Mein
Genre: Cinematic Bollywood Nostalgia / Indie Pop Ballad

[Intro]
Woh subah ki jaldi, woh bhaag ke jaana,
Woh aadha sa homework, bahana banana...
Woh khidki se aati dhoop ka safar,
Woh ghar se bhi pyaara lagta tha ghar...

Haan, ek chhoti si duniya thi,
Jismein saara jahaan tha...

[Antara 1]
Bag mein kitaabein, jeb mein kanche,
Dil mein the kitne iraade,
Teacher ke aate hi chup ho jaana,
Phir peeche se hansna, chhupke se baatein...

Tiffin khula toh sab saath baithe,
Apna bhi aadha, tera bhi aadha,
Jis din jo laaya, sabka hi hota,
Chhota sa lunch bhi lagta tha daawat...

Na kal ki fikrein, na aaj ka darr,
Woh chhoti si baaton pe hansna jee bhar...

[Mukhda / Chorus]
Ek ghanti thi, sau kahaaniyan,
Ek bench thi, poora jahaan,
Hum toh ghar tak aa gaye,
Dil reh gaya classroom mein wahaan...

Woh pencil ke tukde, woh kaagaz ke jahaan,
Woh doston ke chehre, woh beete imtihaan,
Aaj bhi aankhein band karun toh dikhe,
Mera school, mere log, mera bachpan wahaan...

Ek ghanti thi, sau kahaaniyan,
Dil reh gaya classroom mein wahaan...

[Antara 2]
Yaad hai woh din jab baarish mein humne,
Chhutti ke baad bhi bheegna tha chaha,
Geeli si shirt aur mitti ke jooton mein,
Ghar jaake daant bhi hanske tha khaana...

Woh last wali bench pe naam likhna,
Woh ek hi joke pe sau baar hansna,
Woh dost jo bolta, "Kal milte hain phir,"
Kisko pata tha badal jaayega sab kuchh...

Kuchh bade sapne, kuchh kacchi si baatein,
Kuchh chehre jo ab tasveeron mein aate,
Kitne hi saal yun guzarte gaye,
Par woh din kahin dil se guzarte nahin...

[Mukhda / Chorus]
Ek ghanti thi, sau kahaaniyan,

Hum toh ghar tak aa gaye,

[Bridge]
Us aakhri din bhi hum hanse the bahut,
Kisi ne na socha, yeh aakhri milan hai,
Woh bags pe likhe hue waade puraane,
Woh "contact mein rehna" ke saare fasaane...

Tasveer mein sabke chehre toh hain,
Par woh roz milne ke din ab kahaan hain?

Kaash koi darwaaza khul jaaye phir se,
Wahi uniform ho, wahi dost hon paas,
Attendance mein jab mera naam koi le,
Main "Present, ma'am!" bol doon ek baar...

Aur phir se woh ghanti baje...
Aur kuchh bhi na badla ho khaas...

[Instrumental Interlude]

[Final Chorus]
Ek ghanti thi, sau kahaaniyan,
Ek bench thi, poora jahaan,
Waqt humein aage le aaya,
Dil reh gaya classroom mein wahaan...

Woh chhote se sapne, woh befikr samaa,
Woh roothna, manana, woh apna gumaaan,
Zindagi mein sab kuchh dobara mila,
Par na mila woh school wala jahaan...

Ek ghanti thi, sau kahaaniyan,
Kuchh dost the, poora jahaan,
Hum bade ho gaye, zamaana badal gaya,
Dil reh gaya classroom mein wahaan...

[Outro]
Kabhi milna wahin, puraane doston...
Kuchh baatein abhi bhi adhoori hain...

Woh bench ab bhi hogi, woh kamra bhi hoga,
Shaayad kisi aur ka bachpan wahaan hoga...

Aur hum...

Hum toh ghar tak aa gaye...

Dil reh gaya classroom mein wahaan...
