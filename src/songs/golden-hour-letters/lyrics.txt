Title: Golden Hour Letters
Genre & Direction: Dreamy Indie Pop / Nostalgic Acoustic Ballad

[Intro]
(Warm fingerpicked acoustic guitar & mellow upright felt piano, 76 bpm)

[Verse 1]
Found your letter folded in my winter coat,
Every word you typed, every line you wrote.
Dust motes dancing in the amber afternoon,
Funny how a season always leaves too soon.

[Pre-Chorus]
We swore that time would never pull us apart,
Now we're just two heartbeats beating in the dark.

[Chorus]
And the golden hour fades along the kitchen floor,
Leaving quiet shadows where you stood before.
I don't call your number, but I know the rhyme,
Some things stay golden on the other side of time.

[Verse 2]
Passed the old cafe where we would talk till three,
Saw a shadow sitting where we used to be.

[Bridge]
I don't want the anger, I don't keep the score,
I just leave the porch light burning by the door.
If the wind should carry back an old refrain,
I will call it sunshine even in the rain.

[Final Chorus]
And the golden hour fades along the kitchen floor,
Some things stay golden on the other side of time.

[Outro]
On the other side of time... Some things stay golden.
