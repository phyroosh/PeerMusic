Title: Rough Copy (School Crush Song)

[Intro]
(Upbeat acoustic guitar strumming, light whistling, distant school bell chime)

[Mukhda / Chorus]
Rough copy ke aakhri panne pe, tera hi naam likha maine,
Formula saare bhula ke bhi, bas tujhko yaad rakha maine.
Class mein dhyan lagata hoon, par nazrein tujh pe rehti hain,
Dhadkanein meri bina kahe, kitna kuch tujhse kehti hain!
Haan chhutti ki ghanti bajte hi, dil cycle tez chalata hai,
Rough copy ke aakhri panne pe, tera hi naam likha maine.

[Antara 1 / Verse 1]
Woh recess ki thodi jaldi mein, corridor mein takraana tera,
"Sorry" keh kar halke se, nazrein jhuka ke muskuraana tera.
Ik blue ink pen jo maangi thi, maine aaj talak lautayi nahi,
Us din se dosti dhoondhi thi, par aage baat badhayi nahi.
Jab roll call pe tu "Present" kahe, mera dil be-wajah hi jhoome hai,
Tu aage ki bench pe baithi rahe, aur dhoop tere rukh choome hai.

[Mukhda Reprise / Chorus]
Rough copy ke aakhri panne pe, tera hi naam likha maine,
Formula saare bhula ke bhi, bas tujhko yaad rakha maine.
Haan chhutti ki ghanti bajte hi, dil cycle tez chalata hai,
Rough copy ke aakhri panne pe, tera hi naam likha maine.

[Antara 2 / Verse 2]
Cycle stand pe rukta hoon, jab tak tu bag uthati nahi,
Teri bus jab tak mud na jaaye, chain se shaam aati nahi.
Ghar aake kitaabein kholta hoon, homework toh bas bahaana hai,
History ke soye panno mein, bas tera zikr sajaana hai.
Doston ne chheda kitni dafa, main jhoothi kasmein khata hoon,
Par aaine ke aage khade, tujhe "Hi" kehne sharmata hoon!

[Bridge]
Kabhi toh aakhri bench se aage, main himmat karke aaunga,
Tujhe rough copy ka har panna, chupke se dikhlaunga.
Yeh darr bhi hai ke tu hans na de, par umeed bhi thodi baaqi hai,
Tera mud ke ek dafa dekhna, bas itna hi kaafi hai...
Haan itna hi kaafi hai!

[Outro]
Rough copy ke aakhri panne pe...
Tera hi naam likha maine...
Bas maths ke aakhri period mein,
Tujhko hi yaad rakha maine...
(Bicycle bell ding, soft acoustic fade out)
