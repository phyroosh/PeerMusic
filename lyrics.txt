Title: Kacchi Neendein, Pakke Khwaab (Student Life Anthem)

[Intro]
(Warm fingerstyle acoustic guitar picking, soft atmospheric hum)

[Mukhda / Chorus]
Raat ke do baje ki thandi si chai,
Chaar doston ki be-wajah si ladaai.
Deewaron pe chipke umeedon ke panne,
Ghar se bahut door sapne sajaaye.
Yeh kacchi si neendein, yeh pakke se khwaab,
Chhoti si jeb mein bada sa hisaab.
Haan thoda sa darr hai, haan thodi thakan hai,
Magar hauslon ka naya aasmaan hai.
Yeh kacchi si neendein, yeh pakke se khwaab.

[Antara 1 / Verse 1]
Photocopy ke panno mein dooba savera,
Syllabus pahad sa, dil mein basera.
Subah ki attendance, woh aakhri bench,
Assignment ki deadline ka aakhri inch.
Magar canteen ki hansi mein har gam ghula hai,
Ek doosre ke kandhe pe darr sab bhula hai.

[Mukhda Reprise / Chorus]
Yeh kacchi si neendein, yeh pakke se khwaab,
Chhoti si jeb mein bada sa hisaab.
Haan thoda sa darr hai, haan thodi thakan hai,
Magar hauslon ka naya aasmaan hai.
Yeh kacchi si neendein, yeh pakke se khwaab.

[Antara 2 / Verse 2]
Maa ki call aati hai: "Khaana toh khaaya?"
"Haan bilkul theek hoon," jhooth keh ke chupaya.
Kambal mein chup kar jo aansu bahaye,
Subah phir muskaan ban kar woh chhaaye.
Apne hi kandhon pe kal ka yeh bojh hai,
Khud ko talaashne ki ek nayi khoj hai.

[Bridge]
Ek din yeh kamre bhi peeche chhutenge,
Degree milegi, yeh raste battenge.
Paison ki daud mein jab hum ulajhenge,
Inhi be-parwah lamhon ko dhoondhenge...
Haan inhi lamhon ko dhoondhenge!

[Outro]
Hostel ki chhat pe woh taare ginna,
Doston ke saath be-matlab sa hansna.
Yeh kacchi si neendein...
Yeh pakke se khwaab...
(Soft acoustic guitar fade out)
